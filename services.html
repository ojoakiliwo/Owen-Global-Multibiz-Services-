Trade lines

Services and products

Owen Global Multibiz Services sells and supplies physical goods. Prices, pack sizes, and availability are confirmed on quotation because wholesale and agricultural markets move.

Wholesale of goods

We supply goods in bulk to retailers, distributors, and organisations. Typical orders include cartons of packaged foods, beverages, household consumables, and personal-care items.

Retail of goods

We sell goods in smaller quantities to end customers. Retail covers groceries, household essentials, and other everyday merchandise.

Supermarkets

We run supermarket trade: stocking shelves with food, drinks, cleaning products, and daily needs, and serving walk-in and order-based customers.

General goods supply

We fulfil supply requests for mixed merchandise. This includes restocking shops, provisioning offices, and delivering agreed product lists on invoice.

Agriculture

We participate in agricultural trade, including farm produce and related goods. Fresh and perishable items are quoted with handling and delivery terms stated clearly.

How to buy

Send an enquiry with the items and quantities you need. We reply with a quote. Payment can be made through Paystack or another method stated on the invoice.

Product categories we trade

This is the range of physical goods customers can buy or request. Specific brands and SKUs are confirmed per order.

Food and groceries

  • Packaged foods, grains, oils, and staples
  • Beverages and bottled drinks
  • Selected fresh produce where available

Household and personal care

  • Cleaning and laundry products
  • Toiletries and personal-care items
  • General household merchandise

Institutional supply

  • Bulk restocking for shops and kiosks
  • Office and hospitality provisions
  • Mixed general-goods consignments

Agricultural goods

  • Farm produce and commodities
  • Agricultural supplies as available
  • Seasonal harvest lots by quotation

We sell legitimate consumer and agricultural goods. We do not sell prohibited, counterfeit, or restricted items. Returns and refunds are described in our Refund & Return Policy.
