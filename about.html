About Owen Global Multibiz Services

A registered Nigerian trading business serving shops, households, institutions, and farms.

Owen Global Multibiz Services is a business name registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. We trade in goods and agricultural produce. Our work covers wholesale, retail, supermarket operations, and general supply.

What we stand for

Trade should be straightforward. Customers should know who they are dealing with, what they are buying, how they will pay, and how issues are handled. That is why this website states our legal name, our lines of business, our contact details, and our customer policies.

Registration

The business operates under the name Owen Global Multibiz Services, as registered with the CAC. We present this name on invoices, receipts, this website, and payment records so that it matches our official registration and our Paystack merchant profile.

Who we serve

  • Retailers and resellers who need wholesale restocking
  • Households buying groceries and everyday essentials
  • Offices, hospitality, and other organisations that require general goods supply
  • Buyers and partners in agricultural produce and farm-related trade

Payments

We accept payment through Paystack and other agreed methods. Card payments are processed by Paystack, a licensed payments company. We do not store full card details on this website. For disputes, refunds, or order questions, contact us using the details on the Contact page.
