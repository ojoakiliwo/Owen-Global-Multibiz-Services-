Who we are

A registered multibiz trading house.

Owen Global Multibiz Services is registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission of Nigeria. We buy, sell, and move everyday goods — from bulk wholesale lots to supermarket shelves — and we participate in agricultural trade.

Customers can pay securely. We use Paystack to process card and other supported payment methods for local and, once approved, international transactions.
